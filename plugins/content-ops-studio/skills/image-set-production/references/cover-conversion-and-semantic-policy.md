# Cover conversion, thumbnail, and semantic policy

For a lead-generation cover, require a specific business Subject, Audience, Painpoint or Value, account goal, cover objective, approved Cover Copy Package, and supported promise. A generic professional-services Profile returns `COVER_CONTEXT_INSUFFICIENT`; do not compensate with a universal abstract poster.

Every candidate declares a conversion strategy, semantic role, direct relation to the content, text prominence, and text-to-image ratio. Candidates must differ materially in the click argument, business scene, semantic role, or composition—not only palette.

Renderer owns all final Chinese text and produces the 1242×1660 preview plus real 310×414 and 186×248 thumbnails. Check Copy Fidelity, font resolution, Safe Area, overflow, clipping, canvas, file integrity, effective font size, contrast, one-second comprehension, and background competition.

Click Clarity uses 25 points for target-customer clarity, 25 for Painpoint or Value clarity, 20 for one-second comprehension, 20 for thumbnail legibility, and 10 for content-promise alignment. Lead generation requires 85.

Semantic Relevance uses 20 points each for industry, business scene, and Painpoint; 15 each for content Value and Project/Subject; and 10 for Audience recognition. Lead generation requires 80. Decorative-only backgrounds are blocked. Abstract semantics are allowed only when the Project permits them, the relation is explicit, the Audience can recognize it, and the Operator has not rejected it.

All passing results remain `PASS_PENDING_OPERATOR`. Candidate comparison stops at `AWAITING_USER_SELECTION`; never create FPV, G4, Style Lock, remaining pages, or Feishu writes.
