# Cover conversion copy policy

For Xiaohongshu, keep four fields explicit: publish title, primary cover hook, optional secondary cover line, and page-one content copy. They may share claims but must not be treated as interchangeable strings.

Before authoring a lead-generation cover, require a specific Subject, Audience, Painpoint or Value, account goal, decision stage, and supported content promise. If the Subject remains generic, return `COVER_CONTEXT_INSUFFICIENT` and ask one question that identifies the actual business subject, target customer, and recognizable scene.

The primary hook should normally use 6–16 visible characters, never exceed 20, and fit in at most three lines. The secondary line may use at most two lines. Reject paragraph-like cover copy, generic slogans, unsupported outcomes, and copy whose only function is decoration. A Cover Copy Package remains pending G3 until the Operator explicitly approves its exact version.
