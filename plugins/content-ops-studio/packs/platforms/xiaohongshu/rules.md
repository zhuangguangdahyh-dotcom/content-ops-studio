# Xiaohongshu image-post rules

This scaffold defines a 3:4 default aspect ratio, 4–8 pages, six pages by default, and a 20-visible-character publish-title contract. Titles must reflect the content and cannot depend on unsupported claims. Version 1.1.0 adds an explicit conversion-cover layer:

- `publish_title`, `cover_primary_hook`, `cover_secondary_line`, and `page_1_content_copy` are distinct fields.
- Lead-generation covers must name or visibly signal the target customer, Painpoint, Value, Risk, or Decision.
- Primary hooks should use 6–16 visible characters and never exceed 20; up to three lines are allowed.
- Renderer QA must include real 310×414 and 186×248 outputs.
- Click clarity must reach 85/100 and semantic relevance 80/100 with no hard block.
- Decorative-only backgrounds are blocked for lead generation.
- Mechanical or scoring success never creates G3, G4, or aesthetic approval.

The Platform Pack controls platform shape and limits, not industry expertise. Industry Packs remain immutable priors during this update.
