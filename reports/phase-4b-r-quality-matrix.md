# Phase 4B-R quality matrix

| Layer                      | Blocks                                                        | Score role                              | Human gate                     |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Authenticity and integrity | Fake evidence, identity/product/space mismatch, authorization | Hard prerequisite                       | Operator remains authoritative |
| Mechanical                 | Copy, font, crop, overflow, dimensions, file validity         | Contributes through relevant dimensions | Cannot approve G4              |
| Visual                     | Composition, focus, hierarchy, completeness, anti-template    | 100-point model                         | Cannot approve G4              |
| Mode and project fit       | Industry, Subject, Audience, Profile, rejected directions     | Hard block plus weighted fit            | Cannot approve G4              |
| Operator aesthetic         | Explicit APPROVE/REVISE/REJECT                                | Not replaced by score                   | Required                       |

Candidate threshold is 75; formal threshold is 85 with core ratings ≥3; hard blocks always win. Group QA separately evaluates system/identity consistency, meaningful difference, near duplicates, source reuse and contact sheet. Generated-image retries are bounded to three and Renderer retries to two; every attempt is retained.

C-0001 original candidate A/B/C scores of 87/85/89 remain immutable historical evidence. Renderer-complete comparison previews are separately checksum-bound and score 92/94/93 with zero hard blocks. Both generations remain `PASS_PENDING_OPERATOR`; the newer scores do not select or approve a direction.
