## Group shared rules

Use a Renderer-verified modern Chinese serif system and one coherent typography logic. Maintain mature, professional editorial completion. Image and type need an explainable spatial or semantic relationship, and every page visual serves its current Page Intent. Real and AI-generated asset boundaries remain truthful. ## Content-page allowed variations

Content Page type may be materially smaller. Reading, understanding, progression and rhythm take priority. Grid, Composition Family, Crop, Text Region, image/text ratio, density, color area and visual intensity may vary with Page Intent. Image Dominant, Evidence Dominant, Diagnostic Composition and Multi-Evidence Editorial are allowed. The Cover Attention Device is not mandatory on content pages.

## Prohibited deviations

Do not copy the Cover coordinates or impose a fixed top/left title template. White wall, black type, frontal storefront, crop, color, title position and `TYPE_DOMINANT` are not Universal defaults. Do not copy Cover-scale type to content pages, use collisions or low contrast as hierarchy, use irrelevant premium imagery, use ordinary presentation-card layouts, let AI generate formal Chinese, or let an aggregate score offset a Hard Block.
