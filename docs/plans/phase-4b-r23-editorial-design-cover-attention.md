## Visual mass, color and typography as form

Visual mass combines actual bounding-box area, font weight, value/saturation contrast, position, isolation, scale, negative space and image-subject strength. Cover Entry requires a clear primary mass consistent with the chosen Attention Mode.

Color begins with a grayscale hierarchy check. Color may not rescue weak structure. Hue, value, saturation, temperature, quantity and context are recorded without creating a fixed palette or universal accent percentage. Typography shape records line-break shape, text-block shape, edge/scale relation, vertical rhythm and mass distribution without bypassing spatial integrity.
