# Image count and production batches

The default final count is 4–8, commonly 5–6; explicit approved count wins, and Visual/Image Skills cannot change it silently after G3. Every page must carry a distinct information task; summary and CTA pages are optional.

Record `final_page_count`, `source_asset_count`, `direction_candidate_count`, `formal_candidate_count`, `generation_attempt_count`, `failed_attempt_count`, and `retained_history_count`. After direction selection, produce one formal cover. After G4, normally produce remaining pages together; high-consistency-risk modes may test two representative inner pages internally before continuing.
