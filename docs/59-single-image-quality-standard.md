# Single-image quality standard

Five layers apply: authenticity/integrity, mechanical, visual, mode/project fit, and Operator aesthetic approval. Hard blocks override score: copy error, fake evidence, wrong identity, structural or anatomical failure, AI gibberish, low resolution, severe crop, unreadable text, unfinished output, prohibited direction, or authorization failure.

The 100-point weights are semantic fit 20; composition 15; hierarchy 15; asset integrity 15; project/Audience fit 10; uniqueness 10; mode execution 10; mobile performance 5. Candidates require 75; formal assets require 85 and core dimensions ≥3. The result is always pending Operator aesthetic approval.

Quality evidence binds to the exact evaluated asset checksum. A source background score is historical evidence only and cannot be reused for a Renderer-complete direction preview. Copy fidelity, overflow, clipping, font resolution and thumbnail title legibility must be checked again after compositing.
