# Visual references and asset sources

References retain permission status, allowed/prohibited use, style relevance and page relevance; an Evidence asset also binds its Evidence Record. A style reference is never factual proof. Full copyrighted pages/images, absolute paths and unverified permission claims are prohibited in repository artifacts.

Every page declares its source strategy. `GENERATED_BACKGROUND` and `PROGRAMMATIC_GRAPHIC` mean future production is required; neither means an asset exists. Certificates, official marks, logos and screenshots cannot be fabricated. Formal Chinese text is always a Renderer responsibility.
