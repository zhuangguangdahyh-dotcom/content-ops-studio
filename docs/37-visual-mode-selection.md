# Visual Mode selection

Supported modes are SCENE_SERIES, EDITORIAL_SERIES, PRODUCT_LIFESTYLE, EVIDENCE_LED and MIXED. Selection uses content structure, industry, Evidence, authorized assets, Project preferences, rejected directions and Platform constraints. Commercial spaces may prefer SCENE_SERIES but no industry is hard-coded. Professional services can use EDITORIAL_SERIES without people or photo generation.

The decision retains at least three candidates by default, their strengths/limits/risks and a selected executable candidate. A rejected direction or a mode depending on unavailable assets cannot be selected.
