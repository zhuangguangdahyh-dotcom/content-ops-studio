# Content Claim and Evidence Policy

Every material statement is classified as external fact, Project first-party fact, professional judgment, opinion, fictional example or CTA. External and first-party factual Claims require retained Evidence references. `UNSUPPORTED` and `REWRITE_REQUIRED` Claims block G3 readiness.

Professional judgments may omit external Evidence only when they are written as judgments, include their limits and are not disguised statistics, rankings or universal conclusions. Fictional examples must be labeled fictional. Customer results, survey percentages, studies, rankings and cases must never be invented.

Evidence is used at claim level rather than copied as a public-page dump. The package retains Claim text, references, support status, rationale and limitations. A source may support identity or qualification as a basic signal without supporting effectiveness, consultation or transaction outcomes; the copy must preserve that boundary.

The Host may research through its approved research boundary, but the Content Skill never performs raw shell, raw Feishu or unrestricted network calls.
