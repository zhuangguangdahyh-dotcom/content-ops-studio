# Content Angle and Structure

Angle answers which decision lens the Content uses; structure answers how that lens is sequenced. They are independent choices.

Planning produces at least three candidates unless the Operator fixes an angle. Supported angle families include risk, cost, decision, misconception, diagnosis, comparison, process, case, emotional and professional judgment. A fixed angle still undergoes duplication and evidence checks. Changing only a title is not an alternate Content.

The Xiaohongshu Platform Pack supplies stable structures such as checklist, misconception correction, comparison and step sequence. The resolved page count must be 4–8, page numbers must be contiguous, Page 1 must be `COVER`, and every page must have one primary role and one information task.

Phase 3B may preserve a preliminary visual-evidence requirement as handoff context, but `background_direction` and `visual_plan_summary` remain empty. Formal visual decisions belong to the later Visual Planning phase.
