# Formal text and Renderer boundary

Titles, body copy, numbers, data, steps, labels, evidence notes, CTA, page numbers, brand text, footnotes and disclaimers are rendered deterministically. Generated backgrounds do not carry readable informational text. Native text in authorized real evidence may remain with source context.

The Renderer may crop, scale, compose, mask, grade, blur, relight, add geometry and place formal text. It may not change identity, product form, spatial structure, project facts, evidence meaning, approved copy or global visual direction. Programmatic Graphic owns accurate structures and annotations.
