# Content Quality Gate

The Content Quality Report uses nine dimensions whose fixed weights total 100: Focus 15, Audience Relevance 15, Value Delivery 15, Evidence Support 15, Subject Fit 10, Platform Fit 10, Readability 10, Originality 5 and CTA Relevance 5. Dimension scores are 0–5 and the weighted result is deterministic.

G3 readiness requires a score of at least 75 and zero blocking failures. Hard checks cover one confirmed Painpoint, one core problem/viewpoint, legal page count and order, Cover presence, deterministic title length, supported Claims, CTA honesty, forbidden expressions, fabrication, duplication, page-task separation and the Phase 3B visual boundary.

A missing CTA is valid when the Subject cannot genuinely fulfill one. Promotion suitability is a separate editorial judgment and is never inferred from the quality score.
